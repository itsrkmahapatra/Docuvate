Frequently Asked Questions

Why do government portals reject photos and signatures?

Most portal uploaders (UPSC, SSC, State PSC, NTA, Banking) have automated server checks that strictly reject files larger than 50 KB or 20 KB, or files with incorrect aspect ratios. Docuvate precisely rescales the pixel dimensions and performs binary search quality optimization to ensure your image passes the upload validation on the first try.

Can I upload iPhone photos (.HEIC)?

Yes! Docuvate includes client-side HEIC decoding. When you drop an iPhone photo, it is automatically converted into standard JPEG format directly inside your browser without uploading to any server.
